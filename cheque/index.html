Drag a field to move it. Arrow keys nudge 0.5 mm, Shift+arrows 0.1 mm. Positions are millimetres from the top-left corner of the leaf.

First time here? Three steps.

  1. Measure your cheque leaf and enter its size under Settings.
  2. Print the alignment sheet at 100% and lay it over a real cheque.
  3. Switch to Adjust layout and drag each field until it matches.
